Source documents

What each record fills in

A Send Client Letter button is added to the form header of each document below. The client name, address, phone, email and website come from the customer, or from its parent company when the contact has none.

Document Data taken into the letter
CRM / Opportunity Enquiry number, client, related project, opportunity title as scope, next action, notes
Quotation Quotation and CRM numbers, total, payment terms, client LPO / PO, attention, order lines as scope, terms
Estimation (optional add-on) Only with the separate cost estimation add-on: estimate reference, total, payment terms, description, material / labour / overhead breakdown
Customer Invoice Invoice number, origin quotation, CRM number, LPO, total, amount due, payment status, payment terms
Project Project name, linked CRM, contract amount, description, first ten tasks as scope

Workflow

From record to register

STEP 01

Open the wizard

Press Send Client Letter on the opportunity, quotation, invoice or project.

STEP 02

Choose the letter type

Subject, letter, email and WhatsApp text are filled with the source data.

STEP 03

Check and adjust

Edit any field or text, add attachments, and press Generate to refresh the letter.

STEP 04

Send and file

Print PDF, email from the source record, or Save Letter to add it to the register.

Correspondence register

CRM › Documents / Letters › Letters

Questions

Can I write a letter with no source data?

Yes. Pick Blank Letter, or switch off Use Source Defaults, and write freely.

Can users keep their own wording?

Yes. Save as Template stores the edited subject, letter, email and WhatsApp text as a new letter type.

Where is the email sent from?

From the source document, so the email appears in that record's chatter.

Is it really free?

Yes. Setup help and custom templates are available from the CPA Books ERP team.
