The CRN route

Nine stops from the first call to approval

The stage moves on by itself as the site visit is logged, the quotation is sent and confirmed, the work dates are filled and the invoice is posted.

Registration
1. Registered
CRN number, customer, site, contact, complaint type and title.
2. Site Visited
Visited by, date, remarks and the next action.
Quotation
3. Quotation Issued
Create the quotation from the CRN; customer and site are filled in.
4. QTN Approved
Confirmed sales order moves the CRN on.
Execution
5. Work in Progress
Assigned engineer, assign date and end date; material and timesheets are booked.
6. Waiting Invoice
Job finished and ready for billing.
Billing & close
7. Job Complete FOC
Free-of-charge jobs close without an invoice.
8. Job Invoiced
Posted invoice is linked to the CRN.
9. Approved
Approved By is set and the CRN is closed.

Good to know

• On install, all existing projects are marked for complaint registration (with quotations and timesheets allowed), so they can hold CRNs.
• The Complaint Registration app opens on the CRN dashboard and uses its own sidebar menu.
• CRN numbers follow CRN/CO<company>/<year>/00001 and restart every year.
• Admins can load sample CRNs from Settings › FSM Test Data and remove them again.

FAQ

Does it work on Odoo Community?
Yes. It runs on Odoo 14 Community and on Enterprise. No Enterprise app is required.
Can a CRN be closed without a quotation?
Yes. Choose "1. Closed" or "3. FOC" as the next action after the site visit.
Where do material costs come from?
From the CRN's material request lines: the quantity issued on done Material Issue Notes × the product cost.
Can I still create normal project tasks?
Yes. Normal tasks stay as they are; a "Create CRN" button on a task or activity opens a new CRN from it.
