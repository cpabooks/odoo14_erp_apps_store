Contract Progress Billing

Progress invoicing for contractors on Odoo 14. Bill construction work by Work Done % per line, keep Previous % and the contract value, and switch to a Labour Supply invoice when you bill manpower.

Worked example

One progress bill, line by line

The contract items keep their full quantity and price. Work Done % decides how much of each line is billed this time; VAT 5% is calculated on the billed amount.

Item Contract Amount Previous % Work Done % Amount VAT 5% Total
Ductwork, floors 1-10 (Gtee)100,000.0020%30%30,000.001,500.0031,500.00
Chilled water piping (2 x 25,000)50,000.000%50%25,000.001,250.0026,250.00

The journal entry posts income 55,000.00, VAT 2,750.00 and receivable 57,750.00.

What you get

Contracting features on the standard invoice

Labour Supply / Construction switch

A radio choice on customer invoices and credit notes. Manual invoices start as Construction; invoices from sales orders stay normal.

Work Done % on each line

Bills that share of quantity x price. Odoo calculates tax, receivable and income on the result.

Previous %, Contract Amount, Gtee

Earlier billed share, full line value and a guarantee flag stay with the line.

Contract Value total

Shown above the untaxed amount on construction invoices.

Filters and list column

Find Construction or Labour Supply invoices from the invoice list.

Locked after posting

Work Done % and the invoice kind change only on draft invoices.

Construction Invoice print

A progress bill your client can read

Print › Construction Invoice (Work Done %) prints Contract Amount, Previous %, Work Done %, amount, tax and totals on your company layout.

The standard invoice print still works and does not show the work done as a discount.

Questions

Does the accounting match the printed bill?

Yes. Work Done % goes through Odoo's own line calculation, so the journal entry, VAT and amount due equal the invoice.

What happens to discounts?

Labour Supply invoices keep the normal discount column. Construction invoices use Work Done % in its place.

Is Previous % filled automatically?

No. Enter it on each bill; it is shown on the invoice and the print.

Is it really free?

Yes. Setup help is available from the CPA Books ERP team.
